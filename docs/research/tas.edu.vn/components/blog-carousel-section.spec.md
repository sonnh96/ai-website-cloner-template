# BlogCarouselSection Specification

## Overview
- **Target file:** `src/components/blog-carousel-section.tsx`
- **Interaction model:** click-driven carousel (Swiper.js on the live site). Use the shared `Carousel` primitive at `@/components/carousel` (already built — `arrowVariant="circle"`). Do not reimplement carousel mechanics.

Each slide is a single large card: navy (`bg-tas-navy`) background photo card with the article photo as a full-bleed background (`object-cover`) and a dark gradient overlay at the bottom, white bold title text and white/80 excerpt text sitting over the gradient at the bottom of the card. `rounded-lg overflow-hidden`, aspect roughly `16/7` on desktop.
